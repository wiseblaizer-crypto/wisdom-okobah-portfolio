02 / MY STORY THE JOURNEY SO FAR

BEHIND THE WORK

Curiosity brought me here. Helping people gives it purpose.

In 2021, I started learning web development because I genuinely loved websites.

I wanted to understand how they worked and how to build them myself. Most of my learning came through self-study, supported by YouTube tutorials and online courses.

My first client came through Fiverr, where I was hired to redesign an existing website. It was an opportunity to apply what I'd learned to a real client's project.

Today, I'm a freelance web developer and digital creative. I enjoy the challenge of finding practical solutions and creating something useful for someone else.

I like solving problems. I like helping people. Web development gives me a way to do both.

04 / MY APPROACH THINKING BEHIND THE WORK

HOW I THINK

Start with people. Build with purpose.

I enjoy finding practical answers to real problems. These are the principles I aim to bring to every website I work on.

↗ The right solution matters more than the fanciest tool.

  1. 01

    Understand the need.

    Before thinking about the design, I want to know who the website is for and what it needs to help them accomplish.

  2. 02

    Make it useful.

    Clear layouts, responsive experiences, and thoughtful functionality matter more to me than decoration for its own sake.

  3. 03

    Keep getting better.

    Learning has shaped my journey since 2021. I value feedback, practice, and improving with each new challenge.
