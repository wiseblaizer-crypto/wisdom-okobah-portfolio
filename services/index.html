GOOD TO KNOW / FAQ

Questions? I've got answers.

Everything you need to know before we start building something remarkable together.

Have another question? ↗
How much does a website cost?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on your goals, the number of pages, required features, and overall complexity. Share your project details, and I'll provide a tailored quotation.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the project's scope, features, and the availability of content and feedback. I'll discuss an estimated timeline with you before we begin.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Absolutely. I can help improve your website's appearance, navigation, responsiveness, and overall user experience while respecting your existing brand identity.

Which website platforms do you work with?

I work with platforms including Shopify, Wix, and Squarespace, and can also develop custom website experiences. I'll recommend an approach based on your business requirements.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design is a core part of my approach. I design websites to adapt to different screen sizes, including phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes, ongoing updates, maintenance, and support can be discussed depending on your needs and the agreed project scope.

What do you need from me to get started?

A description of your business, your project goals, and any existing brand materials is a great start. We can discuss content, design preferences, and other requirements during our initial conversation.
