Frequently Asked Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Starting a website project comes with questions. Here are a few things you might want to know before we work together.

Have another question? Let's talk. ↗
How much does a website cost?

Website pricing depends on the size, features, and requirements of your project. After discussing what you need, I can provide a tailored quote.

How long does a website project take?

The timeline depends on the scope of work, the features involved, content readiness, and feedback. We'll discuss a realistic schedule before the project begins.

Which website platforms do you work with?

I work with Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, and GoDaddy. The right platform depends on your business goals, the type of website you need, and how you plan to manage it.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I can help improve an existing website's appearance, structure, mobile experience, and usability while keeping your business needs in focus.

Do I need to provide the website content?

It helps to have your business information, branding materials, images, and service or product details ready. If you're unsure what content is needed, we can discuss the requirements together.

Can you help with an online store?

Yes. I offer ecommerce website design and store setup, including Shopify projects. We can discuss your product catalog, store structure, and required functionality to determine the scope.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design is an important part of my approach. I design with different screen sizes in mind so visitors can navigate and use the website across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

Do you provide support after launch?

I offer website updates and ongoing support. The specific support arrangement depends on your project's needs and what we agree on before or after launch.
